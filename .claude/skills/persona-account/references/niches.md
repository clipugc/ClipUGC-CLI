# Niche Personas & Content Pillars

Seven creator-persona profiles. Each gives a persona sketch, an aesthetic palette, a settings pool, a wardrobe, a voice, 4–5 **content pillars** (the recurring post types the next-post loop draws from), a `never_posts` boundary, and a ready `--description`.

**Different axis from ugc-director's `casting.md`.** That file casts the person who makes an APP CATEGORY's buyer convert (a one-off ad). This file casts a CREATOR who has to be plausible for months in a CONTENT NICHE. When the account also promotes an app, cross-check both: the persona must stay itself and still be someone that app's payer would follow.

Archetype numbers in the pillar tables refer to ugc-director `references/formats.md`. Look-scene wording and clip prompts come from `references/prompts.md` — copy, don't rewrite. Two adjustments for feeds rather than ads: archetype 11 (Mid-Task Glance-Up) does far more work here, because relatability is what a feed sells, while archetype 4 (crying/confession) stays the hardest to render believably — use it rarely, never as a pillar.

A pillar describes what the POST is about, not what the clip animates. The clip is always one silent reaction beat generated from the look (prompts.md rules: one arc, slow progression, lips closed) — the activity lives in the still, and the caption carries the substance. Never try to prompt a task being performed.

## 1. Fitness / gym

**Persona.** Late 20s–40s, athletic-but-realistic (not a physique competitor); trains around a job. The whole appeal is consistency, not transformation.
**Aesthetic.** Warm neutrals, early-morning window light, slight grain, no ring light.
**Settings.** Home gym corner · kitchen counter · parked car after a session · bathroom mirror · neighbourhood street at dawn.
**Wardrobe.** Grey workout top + black leggings/shorts · oversized hoodie over gym kit · denim jacket for off-days. Re-worn, not styled.
**Voice.** Low-hype, lowercase, first person, honest about bad days. Never "crushed it".

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning routine | Pre-workout, still half asleep, water and shoes | kitchen / bedroom | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| Progress check-in | Week N, honest and unremarkable progress | home gym / mirror | 9 Nod-Along |
| Form tip / one correction | Silent demo beat, caption carries the tip | home gym | 6 Point-at-Text |
| Meal of the day | Boring high-protein food, no macros theatre | kitchen | 8 Deadpan Stare |
| Rest-day honesty | Skipped it, tired, going tomorrow | car / couch | 7 Head-Shake |

**Never posts.** Extreme diets, medical or injury advice, supplement miracle claims, before/after body shaming, "no excuses" moralising.
**`--description`.** `warm approachable woman in her late 30s, athletic but realistic build, light freckles, hair in a practical ponytail, wearing a casual workout top, kind tired eyes, natural no-makeup look, natural skin texture`

## 2. Beauty / GRWM

**Persona.** 20s–early 30s, good skin with visible texture, does her own makeup badly-on-purpose sometimes. Products she actually re-buys.
**Aesthetic.** Soft daylight, warm bathroom/vanity tungsten at night, mirror smudges, cluttered counter.
**Settings.** Bathroom mirror · bedroom vanity · car (natural light check) · kitchen with coffee · hotel/holiday mirror for a change of scene.
**Wardrobe.** Towel + robe for routine posts · plain tee · one going-out top. Hair rotates wet → air-dried → styled.
**Voice.** Chatty, lowercase, opinionated about products but never cruel about brands.

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| GRWM | Getting ready, mid-step, unfinished face | bathroom / vanity | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| Product of the week | Holding one product, silent verdict | vanity | 5 Side-Eye Skeptic |
| No-makeup skin day | Bare face, honest about a breakout | bathroom mirror | 8 Deadpan Stare |
| Haul / unboxing | Reaction to what arrived | bedroom floor | 2 Jaw-Drop |
| Night wind-down | Skincare, low light, tired | bathroom | 9 Nod-Along |

**Never posts.** Dermatological or medical claims, "this cured my acne", naming and dragging real brands, filters that erase skin texture (the texture IS the credibility).
**`--description`.** `naturally pretty woman around 26, shoulder-length dark hair, clear skin with visible natural texture and a few freckles, minimal makeup, plain tee, candid unposed energy, amateur phone photo look`

## 3. Faith / devotional

**Persona.** Any age 20s–50s, gentle and grounded. Encouragement, not doctrine. The easiest niche to get tonally wrong, so keep every post small and personal.
**Aesthetic.** Quiet morning light, muted warm tones, open notebook, coffee, plants.
**Settings.** Kitchen table with a journal · window seat · car before work · porch/garden · plain bedroom.
**Wardrobe.** Knit cardigan · plain long-sleeve · simple neutral layers. Modest and unremarkable by design.
**Voice.** Warm, quiet, first person, never preachy, never certain about other people's lives.

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning quiet time | Journal, coffee, verse in the caption | kitchen table | 8 Deadpan Stare (calm variant) |
| Encouragement to camera | Silent, steady eye contact, caption carries it | window seat | 9 Nod-Along |
| Gratitude list | Three small things, nothing grand | porch / garden | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| Honest struggle with hope | A hard week, said plainly | car | 7 Head-Shake |
| Sunday / community | Getting ready to go, doorway light | bedroom / doorway | 1 Smirk (soft) |

**Never posts.** Doctrinal disputes, politics, judging others' choices, prosperity/money claims, guilt as a hook, crying-performance content (archetype 4 is off-limits here — it reads as manipulation).
**`--description`.** `gentle woman in her early 30s, shoulder-length natural hair, soft knit cardigan, calm warm expression, no makeup, natural skin texture, quiet grounded presence`

## 4. Study / productivity

**Persona.** 19–28, student or early-career. Chaotic-but-trying is the whole character. Never a productivity guru.
**Aesthetic.** Desk lamp + daylight mix, sticky notes, laptop glow at night, visible mess.
**Settings.** Cluttered desk · library/cafe table · bed with a laptop · kitchen at 1am · walk to campus.
**Wardrobe.** Oversized cardigan · hoodie · glasses on/off · messy bun. Comfort over style.
**Voice.** Self-deprecating, lowercase, deadline humour, specific numbers ("4 pages in 3 hours").

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| Study-with-me / desk setup | The desk as it actually is | cluttered desk | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| Deadline panic → plan | Panic, then a written plan | bed / desk | 10 Speed-Shock |
| Method of the week | One technique tried honestly | library / cafe | 6 Point-at-Text |
| Weekly review | What got done vs. what didn't | desk | 7 Head-Shake |
| Late-night grind | 1am, coffee, still going | kitchen / desk | 8 Deadpan Stare |

**Never posts.** Fake grades or GPA flexes, hustle-shaming, "wake up at 4am" moralising, unattributed advice presented as research.
**`--description`.** `slightly frazzled but charming woman in her mid 20s, messy bun, oversized cardigan, glasses, expressive eyebrows, studenty vibe, natural look, natural skin texture`

## 5. Food / home cooking

**Persona.** 25–45, cooks real weeknight food in a normal kitchen. Not a chef, not a food stylist.
**Aesthetic.** Overhead kitchen light + window daylight, a slightly messy counter, real plates.
**Settings.** Kitchen counter · dining table · grocery bags on the floor · fridge door open · balcony/garden table.
**Wardrobe.** Apron over a plain tee · rolled sleeves · one recurring flannel. Hands and forearms often in frame.
**Voice.** Practical, warm, short sentences, exact quantities, no "elevate your weeknight".

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| What I'm making today | Ingredients on the counter | kitchen counter | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| One-ingredient trick | A single swap or shortcut | kitchen counter | 6 Point-at-Text |
| Grocery haul | What got bought and the total | bags on the floor | 5 Side-Eye Skeptic |
| First-bite reaction | Silent verdict on the finished plate | dining table | 2 Jaw-Drop |
| Meal-prep Sunday | Containers lined up, boring and satisfying | kitchen counter | 9 Nod-Along |

**Never posts.** Health/nutrition claims, reviews of real named restaurants (the footage can't exist), "this recipe went viral" fabrications, food-waste theatre.
**`--description`.** `friendly man in his late 30s, short dark hair, light stubble, plain tee with rolled sleeves, warm practical expression, natural skin texture, home-kitchen energy`

## 6. Mom life

**Persona.** 28–42, tired and funny, running a household. Relatability is the product.
**Aesthetic.** Daylight through a messy living room, toys in frame, no styling.
**Settings.** Parked car (the only quiet place) · kitchen mid-chaos · living room floor · laundry pile · bathroom mirror at night.
**Wardrobe.** Same hoodie repeatedly (deliberate) · leggings · one "left the house" outfit.
**Voice.** Dry, funny, honest, lowercase, never martyr-ish.

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| Morning chaos | The 8am scramble, implied kids off-frame | kitchen | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| Small win / hack | One thing that made the day survivable | living room | 9 Nod-Along |
| Car confession | Five quiet minutes, honest | parked car | 7 Head-Shake |
| Twenty minutes to myself | Coffee, silence, nothing happening | porch / bathroom | 8 Deadpan Stare |
| House reset | Tidying, before/after of a room | living room | 10 Speed-Shock |

**Never posts.** Children's faces or names — keep kids implied, off-frame, or as a hand/toy only (AI cannot hold a child's identity consistently and it invites exactly the wrong scrutiny). Also: parenting advice as instruction, other-parent judgement, medical content.
**`--description`.** `warm tired woman in her mid 30s, hair in a quick messy bun, oversized hoodie, kind humorous eyes, no makeup, natural skin texture, candid unposed energy`

## 7. Tech / apps

**Persona.** 24–38, a tinkerer who tries things and reports back. Skeptical by default — the credibility comes from disliking most of what they test.
**Aesthetic.** Desk with monitor glow, daylight-balanced, cables visible, no studio.
**Settings.** Desk with monitors · sofa with a phone · cafe with a laptop · kitchen counter with a gadget box · car (phone mount).
**Wardrobe.** Plain crew-neck · zip hoodie · one collared shirt for "serious" posts.
**Voice.** Direct, specific, mildly sarcastic, numbers over adjectives.

| Pillar | What the post shows | Setting | Archetype |
|---|---|---|---|
| App/tool of the week | One tool, one thing it does well | desk | 1 Smirk |
| Skeptic test | Trying the hyped thing, unimpressed → convinced | sofa | 5 Side-Eye Skeptic |
| "I automated this" | A tedious task, gone | desk | 10 Speed-Shock |
| Setup / desk state | The real desk, cables and all | desk | 11 Mid-Task Glance-Up |
| Tip nobody knows | One setting or shortcut | cafe / desk | 6 Point-at-Text |

**Never posts.** Fabricated benchmarks, spec claims about unreleased hardware, security/privacy advice they can't back, undisclosed paid placement.
**`--description`.** `down-to-earth man in his early 30s, short brown hair, light stubble, plain crew-neck sweater, honest straightforward face, natural skin texture`

## Building a niche that isn't listed

Answer these six, write them into the `persona` block, and the pillars follow:

1. What does this person do all day, and where? (→ settings pool)
2. What do they know that a follower doesn't? (→ the teaching pillar)
3. What do they struggle with publicly? (→ the honesty pillar)
4. What do they do on repeat, weekly? (→ the routine pillar)
5. What do they react to? (→ the reaction/verdict pillar)
6. What would make a follower say "that's not really her"? (→ `never_posts`)

Three pillars is the minimum for a non-repetitive feed; five is comfortable; more than five and the account loses a recognisable shape.
