# Reaction Library: the emotional palette

`formats.md` has the **12 conversion archetypes** — the shock/curiosity/approval beats that
sell an app in a 15–30s ad. This file is the wider **emotional palette**: ~30 silent
reactions across 9 emotion families, for persona accounts and for niches where an ad-shaped
"jaw-drop at a feature" would ring false.

Use this when:

- running a **persona account** (`persona-account` skill) — a grid of 30 posts cannot be 30
  jaw-drops, and the accounts that work rotate feeling, not just setting,
- the niche is **relational or emotional** — long-distance couples, dating, faith, mom-life,
  mental health, study-motivation — where longing, sulking and giggling convert better than
  disbelief,
- you have **already used the obvious archetype** on this account and need the next post to
  feel like a different day rather than a re-shoot.

Every prompt obeys the golden rules in `prompts.md`: no talking, staged progression, slow
verbs, anchored hands, ambient motion, held ending. Copy them verbatim into
`clipugc videos create --prompt "…"`. All are written for `--duration 5`.

Swap pronouns freely; the look image decides who the person is, not the prompt.

## How the reference accounts actually use these

Study a working AI/UGC creator grid (e.g. a long-distance-relationship app account) and the
pattern is consistent:

- **One face, one wardrobe, many feelings.** The person and outfit barely change across a
  whole grid; what changes post to post is the emotion and the hook text.
- **The hook carries the story, the face carries the feeling.** The reaction is never
  literal — she is not miming "distance tracker", she is reacting to *being told* about it.
  That gap between overlay text and expression is the format.
- **The same hook gets several different reactions.** The same line ("4 YEARS doing long
  distance and I just found out about THIS") is re-shot as shock, as sulking, as delight.
  Re-merging is free in ClipUGC, so this is the cheapest A/B you have — one clip, several
  hooks, or one hook across several reactions.
- **Soft emotions outnumber loud ones.** Roughly two thirds of a relational grid is
  affection, longing, sulk and cheek-rest; jaw-drops are the punctuation, not the sentence.

## 1. Shock & disbelief

The loud end. Covered in depth by archetypes 2, 3, 10 in `prompts.md` — these are extra
variants so a grid can carry more than one.

**Wide-eye freeze** — smallest, most repeatable shock:
```
Handheld selfie framing with a tiny natural wobble. Her eyes gradually widen and her brows lift as she reads something just off camera, her expression freezing in place, lips pressed together, then she blinks once and holds the stunned look at the lens. Hair moves subtly. No talking, mouth stays closed.
```

**Both-hands-to-cheeks** — bigger, still believable:
```
Handheld selfie framing. Her expression shifts slowly from neutral to astonished — eyebrows rising, eyes widening — and she brings both hands up to rest flat against her cheeks, fingers still, framing her face, holding the stunned expression at the camera. Hair moves subtly. No talking, mouth stays closed.
```

**Slow lean-in** — curiosity tipping into disbelief:
```
Handheld selfie framing. She leans slowly toward the camera as if trying to read something small, eyes narrowing slightly, then her eyebrows shoot up in realisation and she pulls back with a silent open-mouthed gasp, holding it. Hair follows the motion naturally. No talking.
```

## 2. Curiosity & secrecy

**Raised-brow tease** — "there's something you don't know":
```
Handheld selfie framing with a tiny wobble. She holds eye contact, one eyebrow slowly raising, the corner of her mouth curling into a small closed-mouth smile, then she tilts her head slightly and holds the look. Hair moves subtly. No talking, lips stay closed.
```

**Glance-away-then-back** — conspiratorial:
```
Handheld selfie framing. She glances quickly off to the side as if checking nobody is listening, then looks back at the lens with a closed-mouth grin and raises her eyebrows twice, holding the expression. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Finger-to-lips** — the secret:
```
Handheld selfie framing, locked composition. She looks directly into the camera and slowly raises one index finger to her closed lips in a "keep this quiet" gesture, hand steady, eyes smiling, holding the pose at the end. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

## 3. Affection & longing

The core of a relationship-niche grid, and the most under-used family in ad work.

**Dreamy cheek-rest** — the single most reusable soft beat:
```
Nearly static selfie framing with subtle handheld drift. She rests her cheek against her palm, elbow anchored, eyes softening as a slow warm closed-mouth smile spreads, gaze drifting slightly off camera then returning to the lens, holding it. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Lovesick sigh:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. Her gaze softens and drifts upward, she exhales slowly through her nose, shoulders dropping, then her eyes return to the camera with a small wistful closed-mouth smile that she holds. Hair moves subtly. No talking, mouth stays closed.
```

**Hugging the phone** — longing, no words:
```
Handheld selfie framing. She looks at the camera with a soft sad-happy expression, then slowly draws the phone toward her chest in a small hug, eyes closing briefly, before easing back to the lens with a gentle closed-mouth smile. Motion is slow and steady. No talking.
```

**Blown kiss** — anchored so the hand does not warp:
```
Handheld selfie framing. She smiles softly at the lens, brings her fingertips to her closed lips in a slow controlled motion, then moves the hand a short distance outward and stills it, keeping eye contact and a warm smile. Fingers stay together. No talking.
```

**Blushing look-away:**
```
Handheld selfie framing with a tiny wobble. A shy closed-mouth smile grows on her face, she breaks eye contact and looks down and away, tucking a strand of hair behind her ear with a natural grip, then glances back at the lens still smiling. No talking.
```

## 4. Sulk & sadness

The reference grids lean on these far more than ad work does. Keep them 5s — sadness drifts
fastest.

**Pouting sulk:**
```
Nearly static selfie framing with subtle drift. Her mouth settles into a small closed pout, eyebrows drawing together slightly, eyes fixed on the lens in a quiet complaint, then she blinks slowly and holds the sulking expression. Hair moves subtly. No talking, lips stay closed.
```

**Chin-down, eyes-up** — vulnerable:
```
Nearly static selfie framing. She lowers her chin slowly while keeping her eyes on the camera, brows lifting slightly in the middle, expression turning soft and sad, holding the look without moving. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Quiet tearing-up** — hardest; only with a still, close look:
```
Static selfie shot with subtle handheld drift. Her eyes gradually glisten, she blinks once slowly as the emotion rises, presses her lips together into a trembling closed-mouth smile and nods faintly, holding it. Hair moves subtly. No talking, no sobbing, no sudden movement.
```

**Blanket cocoon** — sad-cozy, works in a bed look:
```
Nearly static framing. She pulls the blanket slowly up toward her chin, only her face visible, eyes on the camera with a small tired sad expression, then blinks slowly and holds it. Motion is minimal and slow. No talking.
```

## 5. Playful & cheeky

**Suppressed giggle:**
```
Handheld selfie framing with a tiny wobble. She tries to hold a straight face, her lips pressing together and twitching, cheeks lifting, until a closed-mouth grin breaks through and her shoulders shake once with a silent laugh, holding the smile. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Slow wink:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. She holds eye contact with a small closed-mouth smile, then closes one eye in a slow deliberate wink and reopens it, the smile widening slightly, holding the look at the lens. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Tongue-in-cheek smug:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. Her mouth pushes slightly to one side in a smug closed-mouth expression, one eyebrow lifting, then she nods once slowly as if pleased with herself and holds the look. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Mischief lean:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. She leans slowly toward the camera with a growing closed-mouth grin, eyes bright and playful, eyebrows lifting, stopping close to the lens and holding the mischievous look. Hair follows naturally. No talking.
```

## 6. Sass & judgment

**Unimpressed slow blink:**
```
Nearly static selfie framing. She stares directly into the camera with a flat unimpressed expression, blinks once very slowly, one eyebrow rising a fraction, and holds the deadpan look without smiling. Hair moves subtly. No talking, lips stay closed.
```

**Side-eye judgment** — no redemption beat, unlike archetype 5:
```
Handheld selfie framing. She slowly slides her eyes to the side without turning her head, holding a flat judging expression, then returns her gaze to the lens with one eyebrow raised and holds it. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Eye-roll:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. Her eyes roll slowly upward and back down in exasperation, head tilting a fraction, ending with a small resigned closed-mouth smile at the camera. Motion is smooth and unhurried. No talking.
```

**Arms-crossed wait:**
```
Handheld framing, locked composition. She folds her arms slowly across her chest, settles her weight, and fixes the camera with a patient unimpressed look, one eyebrow raised, holding it. Arms stay still once folded. No talking.
```

## 7. Anxious & waiting

**Nail-bite worry:**
```
Handheld selfie framing with a tiny wobble. Her eyes flick down and back up, brows drawing together, and she raises one hand to bring a fingertip near her closed lips, holding the anxious look at the camera. Hand stays steady, fingers together. No talking.
```

**Checking-the-phone glance:**
```
Handheld framing, slight wobble. She glances down and off camera as if checking a notification, her expression falling slightly, then she looks back at the lens with a small disappointed closed-mouth smile and holds it. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Impatient exhale:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. She exhales slowly through her nose, cheeks puffing very slightly, eyes drifting up and then back to the camera with a flat waiting expression, holding it. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

## 8. Warmth & approval

**Proud smile:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. A closed-mouth smile grows slowly and reaches her eyes, her chin lifting a fraction, then she nods once, satisfied, and holds the warm expression at the lens. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Relieved exhale:**
```
Handheld selfie framing. Her tense expression softens, she exhales slowly through her nose and her shoulders drop, a small relieved closed-mouth smile forming as she holds eye contact. Hair moves subtly. No talking.
```

**Heart-hands** — anchored:
```
Handheld selfie framing. She smiles warmly at the lens and slowly brings both hands together in front of her chest to form a small heart shape, fingers steady once joined, holding the pose and the smile. No talking.
```

## 9. Tired & cozy

**Sleepy blink:**
```
Nearly static framing, minimal motion. Her eyes are heavy, she blinks slowly twice, a small tired closed-mouth smile forming, then she settles her head slightly and holds the sleepy look at the camera. No talking.
```

**Suppressed yawn:**
```
Nearly static selfie framing. She raises the back of one hand toward her closed mouth as a yawn passes, eyes squeezing shut briefly, then relaxes and looks back at the camera with a soft tired smile. Hand steady. No talking.
```

**Waking-up squint:**
```
Nearly static framing in bed. She squints against the light, eyes opening slowly, a small sleepy closed-mouth smile forming as she focuses on the camera, holding it. Motion is minimal. No talking.
```

## Choosing one

- **Ad, feature reveal** → shock, curiosity, approval (`formats.md` archetypes 1–3, 5–10).
- **Relationship / dating / faith / mom-life account** → affection, sulk, playful, cozy.
- **Complaint or relatable-pain hook** → sass, anxious, sulk.
- **Proof / result hook** → warmth & approval, shock.

Grid discipline: never run the same family twice in a row, and keep loud reactions (shock,
big playful) to roughly one in three. A feed of nothing but jaw-drops reads as a bot; a feed
of nothing but soft smiles reads as a stock library. The rotation is the realism.
